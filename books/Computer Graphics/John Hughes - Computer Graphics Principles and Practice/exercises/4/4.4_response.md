### Answer 4.4

1. **Grid:** Draw a rotating field of crosses or dots at a fixed angular velocity $\omega$.
2. **Targets:** Place small colored disks at specific grid locations.
3. **Experiment:**
* Increase grid density (spacing) to increase the likelihood of MIB.
* Vary color contrast between the grid and the target dots; MIB is often stronger when targets have low chromatic contrast with the background.
* Adjust $\omega$; the effect is usually strongest at specific angular speeds that disrupt sustained visual fixation.
