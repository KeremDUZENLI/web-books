### Exercise 4.2

Modify the corner-cutting program to be a “dualizing” program: It replaces a polygon with its dual, a polygon whose vertices are the midpoints of the original polygon, connected in the same order, so that the dual of a square is a diamond. Experiment: Does repeated dualizing tend to remove crossings of self-intersecting polygons? Can you find a polygon whose successive duals are always self-intersecting?
