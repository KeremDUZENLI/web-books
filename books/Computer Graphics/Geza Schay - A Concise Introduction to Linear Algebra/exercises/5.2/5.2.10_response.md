### Answer 5.2.10

**Plane $S$:** Normal vector $\mathbf{n} = (2, 3, -1)^T$. $\|\mathbf{n}\|^2 = 4 + 9 + 1 = 14$.

**a. Projection Matrices:**
$P_{\mathbf{n}} = \frac{\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T}{14} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 & -1 \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 & -2 \\ 6 & 9 & -3 \\ -2 & -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
$P_S = I - P_{\mathbf{n}} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 14 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 14 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 14 \end{bmatrix} - \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 & -2 \\ 6 & 9 & -3 \\ -2 & -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 10 & -6 & 2 \\ -6 & 5 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 13 \end{bmatrix}$.

**b. Sum:**
$P_S + P_{\mathbf{n}} = P_S + (I - P_S) = I$. Checked.

**c. Decomposition of $\mathbf{x} = (2, 3, 4)^T$:**
$\mathbf{x}_\perp = P_{\mathbf{n}} \mathbf{x} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 & -2 \\ 6 & 9 & -3 \\ -2 & -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 18 \\ 27 \\ -9 \end{bmatrix} = (9/7, 27/14, -9/14)^T$.
$\mathbf{x}_S = \mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_\perp = (2, 3, 4)^T - (18/14, 27/14, -9/14)^T = (5/7, 15/14, 65/14)^T$.

**d. Distance:**
Distance $= \|\mathbf{x}_\perp\| = \frac{1}{14} \sqrt{18^2 + 27^2 + (-9)^2} = \frac{1}{14} \sqrt{1134} = \frac{9\sqrt{14}}{14} \approx 2.41$.
